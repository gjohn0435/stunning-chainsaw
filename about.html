ABOUT NEO-BRUTALISM

Understanding The Raw, Honest Design Movement

WHAT IS NEO-BRUTALISM?

Neo-Brutalism is a design movement that rejects polish, smoothness, and corporate aesthetics. Inspired by Brutalist architecture's "béton brut" (raw concrete), it brings honesty, rawness, and anti-establishment rebellion to digital design.

CORE PHILOSOPHY: Show what things really are. Don't hide structure. Embrace roughness. Function over decoration.

Where Swiss design is refined and elegant, Neo-Brutalism is deliberately rough. Where minimalism is subtle, Brutalism is harsh. Where corporate design is smooth, Brutalism is raw.

THE FIVE PRINCIPLES OF NEO-BRUTALISM

01

TRUTH TO MATERIALS

Don't hide what things are made of. In architecture, this meant exposed concrete. In web design, it means raw HTML, visible grid structures, and system fonts. No fake textures, no decorative overlays, no pretense.

EXAMPLE: Use Arial instead of custom webfonts. Show the grid instead of hiding it. Expose the structure of the page.

02

HONEST STRUCTURE

Make the skeleton visible. Brutalist buildings show their support beams and plumbing. Neo-Brutalist websites show their grids, borders, and layout logic. Nothing is seamless—everything is compartmentalized and visible.

EXAMPLE: Thick borders (4-5px) around sections. Visible grid lines. Boxy, compartmentalized layouts.

03

ANTI-CORPORATE REBELLION

Reject smooth, polished, "safe" design. Brutalism is deliberately ugly, purposefully rough. It signals authenticity, honesty, and anti-establishment values. A mainstream brand would reject it—that's the point.

EXAMPLE: Hard shadows instead of subtle blur. Instant state changes instead of smooth transitions. Bright yellow instead of tasteful pastels.

04

FUNCTIONAL MINIMALISM

Every element must have a purpose. Decoration is dishonest. If it doesn't serve function, it doesn't belong. This isn't about simplicity for aesthetics—it's about simplicity for honesty.

EXAMPLE: No decorative illustrations. No unnecessary animations. No visual flair without function.

05

RAWNESS AS VIRTUE

Roughness is not a flaw to fix—it's a feature to embrace. Tight spacing creates tension. Harsh contrasts demand attention. Unrefined typography feels authentic. Polish hides truth; rawness reveals it.

EXAMPLE: Tight line-height (1.2-1.3). ALL CAPS headings. Packed layouts. No generous white space.

VISUAL CHARACTERISTICS

COLOR

Stark Black & White

Primary: #000000, #FFFFFF

Accent: Bright yellow (#FFFF00), red (#FF0000), blue (#0000FF)

No gradients. No subtle variations. High contrast always.

TYPOGRAPHY

System Fonts Only

Arial, Courier, Helvetica

ALL CAPS for headings. Tight line-height (1.2-1.3). Large size contrast (huge headings, modest body).

BORDERS

Thick & Black

3-5px solid black borders around everything. No rounded corners. Sharp 90° angles. Boxy, compartmentalized.

SHADOWS

Hard & Offset

8px 8px 0 #000000

No blur. No subtlety. Offset shadows that create depth without smoothness.

SPACING

Tight & Packed

Minimal padding. Content near edges. Intentional crowding creates tension. Not generous like Swiss design.

MOTION

Instant Changes

No smooth transitions. No ease-in-out. No fades. State changes are abrupt and honest.

WHY NEO-BRUTALISM MATTERS

01. STANDS OUT

In a sea of identical flat design and generic minimalism, Brutalism is unmistakably different. It grabs attention because it refuses to blend in.

02. SIGNALS AUTHENTICITY

Corporate design is smooth and polished. Brutalism is raw and honest. It signals that a brand or creator values truth over appearance.

03. REACTIONS AGAINST TRENDS

Swiss design became corporate standard. Minimalism became Apple standard. Brutalism rejects standards—it's inherently rebellious.

04. FUNCTION-FIRST

Brutalism doesn't waste time on decoration. It's fast to build, easy to maintain, and focused on what matters: content and function.

05. MEMORABLE

You don't forget a Brutalist website. The thick borders, harsh contrasts, and raw aesthetic create lasting impressions.

06. DESIGN LINEAGE

Understanding Brutalism means understanding design history. It connects digital design to architecture, modernism, and punk rebellion.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

This site is part of the Swiss Design Lineage Vibe Coding project for IS 218 - Building Web Applications at NJIT (November 2025).

PROJECT GOALS

  • Transform Swiss-style design into Neo-Brutalist aesthetic
  • Demonstrate understanding of design movements and their lineages
  • Practice strategic AI collaboration for design implementation
  • Build authentic, portfolio-worthy design work

DESIGN TRANSFORMATION

This site deliberately transforms Swiss design principles into their Brutalist opposites:

  • Swiss's invisible grid → Brutalism's exposed structure
  • Swiss's generous white space → Brutalism's tight packing
  • Swiss's refined typography → Brutalism's raw ALL CAPS
  • Swiss's subtle colors → Brutalism's stark black/white
  • Swiss's professional elegance → Brutalism's rough rebellion

THE RESULT: A fully authentic Neo-Brutalist design gallery that demonstrates mastery of the style while maintaining usability, accessibility, and technical quality.
