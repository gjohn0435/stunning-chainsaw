KEY PRACTITIONERS

Architects & Designers Who Shaped The Movement

Neo-Brutalism builds on the legacy of Brutalist architects while adding digital-age rebellion. These practitioners defined what "raw, honest design" means across architecture and web design.

BRUTALIST ARCHITECTS

The Foundation: 1950s-1970s

LE CORBUSIER

1887-1965

Pioneer of Brutalist Architecture

KEY CONTRIBUTION

Coined "béton brut" (raw concrete). Created the philosophical foundation: buildings should be honest about their materials and structure.

NOTABLE WORKS

  • Unité d'Habitation (Marseille, 1952)
  • Notre-Dame du Haut (Ronchamp, 1954)
  • Chandigarh government buildings (India, 1950s)

"A house is a machine for living in. Truth to materials above all."

ALISON & PETER SMITHSON

1928-1993 / 1923-2003

British Brutalism Leaders

KEY CONTRIBUTION

Brought Brutalism to Britain. Emphasized "honest" structure, exposed materials, and anti-decoration philosophy.

NOTABLE WORKS

  • Robin Hood Gardens (London, 1972)
  • Hunstanton School (Norfolk, 1954)
  • Economist Building (London, 1964)

"Brutalism tries to face up to a mass-production society."

ERNŐ GOLDFINGER

1902-1987

Social Housing Brutalist

KEY CONTRIBUTION

Applied Brutalist principles to social housing. Monumental tower blocks as democratic architecture for the masses.

NOTABLE WORKS

  • Trellick Tower (London, 1972)
  • Balfron Tower (London, 1967)
  • Alexander Fleming House (London, 1959)

"Concrete is the material of our time."

PAUL RUDOLPH

1918-1997

American Brutalist Master

KEY CONTRIBUTION

Brought sculptural, dramatic Brutalism to America. Textured concrete surfaces, bold geometric forms.

NOTABLE WORKS

  • Yale Art & Architecture Building (1963)
  • Boston Government Service Center (1971)
  • Orange County Government Center (NY, 1971)

"Architecture should disturb, not reassure."

DIGITAL NEO-BRUTALIST PIONEERS

The Digital Revolution: 2010s-Present

PASCAL DEVILLE

Active 2014-Present

Founder of Brutalist Websites

KEY CONTRIBUTION

Created brutalistwebsites.com in 2014 - THE definitive collection and legitimization of Neo-Brutalism in web design. Catalogued 1000+ sites.

NOTABLE WORKS

  • brutalistwebsites.com (2014-present)
  • "Brutalist Websites" book (2018)
  • Curator and arbiter of digital Brutalism

"Raw, honest web design that rejects corporate polish."

DAVID RUDNICK

Active 2010s-Present

Graphic Designer / Art Director

KEY CONTRIBUTION

Applied Brutalist aesthetics to music industry design. Raw typography, harsh contrasts, anti-corporate rebellion.

NOTABLE WORKS

  • Album covers for electronic music labels
  • Festival branding (Bloc, Unsound)
  • Experimental typography projects

"Design as resistance to smoothness."

BALENCIAGA DIGITAL TEAM

2016-Present

High Fashion Brutalism

KEY CONTRIBUTION

Brought Neo-Brutalism to luxury fashion. Proved that raw, anti-corporate aesthetics could work for high-end brands.

NOTABLE WORKS

  • balenciaga.com redesign (2016)
  • Black/white harsh aesthetics
  • Raw typography, minimal decoration

"Luxury through honesty, not ornament."

EXPERIMENTAL JETSET

Active 1997-Present

Dutch Graphic Design Collective

KEY CONTRIBUTION

Systematic, grid-based design with Brutalist rawness. Influenced web designers to expose structure and embrace simplicity.

NOTABLE WORKS

  • Identity systems for cultural institutions
  • Poster design with raw typography
  • Books on design methodology

"Form follows function, structure shows truth."

SHARED PRINCIPLES ACROSS GENERATIONS

TRUTH TO MATERIALS

Don't hide what things are made of. Concrete in architecture. HTML in web design.

EXPOSED STRUCTURE

Show the grid. Show the support beams. Show the bones of the design.

FUNCTION OVER FORM

Decoration is dishonest. Every element should have purpose.

ANTI-CORPORATE

Reject smooth, polished, safe design. Embrace rough edges and rawness.
